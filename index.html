Poster Printing & Assembly Guide

1

Print at Actual Size (100%)

In your PDF viewer print settings, choose "Actual Size" or scale 100%. Disable "Fit to Page" or "Shrink oversized pages" so sheet boundaries align seamlessly.

2

Trim the Margins

If you configured a margin (e.g. 5–10 mm), use a ruler and craft knife or scissors to cut along the outer white borders.

3

Assemble in Reading Order

Sheets are ordered left-to-right, row-by-row (Page 1 is top-left). Butt adjacent pages together and secure them on the back with tape.
